[SIZE="5"][COLOR="SeaGreen"]APH-Search[/COLOR][/SIZE]
[COLOR="Gray"][i]Search settings, keybinds, add-ons, items, skills, champion stars, the map, quests, achievements, menus and slash commands, and jump straight to what you picked, and you can also find your friends and share waypoints with your group.[/i][/COLOR]

[SIZE="3"][COLOR="DarkOrchid"]Dependencies:[/COLOR][/SIZE]

This addon requires:
[LIST]
[*] [COLOR="#FF69B4"]LibAPH[/COLOR] [COLOR="Gray"][i](Required Unified helpers shared with my addons)[/i][/COLOR]
[/LIST]

Optionals for additional features:
[LIST]
[*] [url="https://www.esoui.com/downloads/info7-LibAddonMenu-2.0.html"][COLOR="#FF69B4"]LibAddonMenu-2.0[/COLOR][/url] [COLOR="Gray"][i](PC Settings Menu)[/i][/COLOR]
[*] [COLOR="#FF69B4"]LibHarvensAddonSettings[/COLOR] [COLOR="Gray"][i](Console Settings Menu)[/i][/COLOR]
[*] [COLOR="#FF69B4"]LibImplex[/COLOR] [COLOR="Gray"][i](Guide arrows on the ground and names in the world)[/i][/COLOR]
[*] [COLOR="#FF69B4"]LibGroupBroadcast[/COLOR] [COLOR="Gray"][i](Shares waypoints and wayshrine travel with your group)[/i][/COLOR]
[*] [COLOR="#FF69B4"]APH-Search Map Data[/COLOR] [COLOR="Gray"][i](Roads and walkable ground for every map, so the guide can follow paths)[/i][/COLOR]
[/LIST]

[b]Without the optional Dependencies:[/b] You can still run the addon entirely independent, and control its settings via built-in slash commands as a standalone utility.

[SIZE="3"][COLOR="DarkOrchid"]Slash Commands[/COLOR][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*] [color=#00FFFF]/fs[/color]: opens the search bar.
[*] [color=#00FFFF]/fsguide[/color]: turns the guide on or off. [color=#00FFFF]/fsguide clear[/color] removes where it is leading you, [color=#00FFFF]/fsguide quest[/color] shares the quest you track with your group, and [color=#00FFFF]/fsguide next[/color] follows the next group member, and [color=#00FFFF]/fsguide record[/color] starts or stops recording a road.
[/LIST]
